TorrentSpire AI is a modern torrent manager with a built-in local AI copilot. It helps you search, evaluate, and manage downloads faster while keeping full control in your hands.

What TorrentSpire AI does:
- Search and add torrents quickly with a dual-pane workflow.
- Paste magnet links directly and start resolving metadata immediately.
- Track download queue progress in real time.
- Keep a local library of completed downloads.

Built-in AI Copilot:
- Ask natural language commands like:
  - "search for ubuntu iso"
  - "download the top result"
  - "what's downloading"
- Receive streaming AI responses in real time.
- Use torrent-aware context so answers match your current search, selection, queue, and library.
- Works with local Ollama models for private, on-device-first AI workflows.

AI Info Cards and Trust Signals:
- Selecting a torrent auto-generates an AI Info Card.
- Cards include a short summary, quality hints, and a Trust Signal:
  - Green: strong indicators
  - Yellow: mixed indicators
  - Red: risky indicators
- Quickly ask follow-up analysis with "Tell me more".

Download management:
- Queue and library views are always visible in the main workspace.
- Download state is updated continuously with periodic status refresh.
- Active torrents are restored on app restart.

Designed for power users:
- Dark, focused interface.
- Resizable dual-pane layout on desktop.
- Fast navigation for search, detail review, and AI chat.

TorrentSpire AI combines practical torrent tooling with context-aware AI guidance so you can decide faster and download smarter.
